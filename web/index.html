Step 2 — Flash payload

Drop your iBSS.boot payload here — built with surrealra1n. It gets compressed with LZ4 in-browser and written to 0x10020000 over USB Serial. Nothing leaves your machine.

LED states

Once flashed the board runs standalone — plug in power, connect the iPhone, done.

Solid Orange
Idle, waiting for a USB device.
Solid Magenta
Recovery mode detected — hold Vol Down + Power. Reboot packet fires at tick 2.
Solid Cyan
Release Power, keep holding Vol Down for ~8 seconds.
Blinking White
Let go of everything. Waiting for DFU.
Solid Blue
USBLiter8 exploit running.
Solid Green
Done — payload sent, iPhone booting.
Waveshare RP2350 USB-A

The USB-A port runs off VSYS and uses GP12/13. The firmware handles the R13 resistor and USB bus resets automatically — nothing to mod.

Cables

Standard USB-A to Lightning between the board and phone. Power the board via USB-C or the 5V pin.
