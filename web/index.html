LED states

The board runs standalone once flashed. Plug in power, connect the iPhone:

  • Orange idle, waiting for USB.
  • Magenta recovery, hold Vol Down + Power.
  • Cyan release Power, keep Vol Down ~8s.
  • Blinking White release all, waiting for DFU.
  • Blue USBLiter8 exploit running.
  • Green done, iPhone booting.

Notes

Waveshare RP2350 USB-A
USB-A runs off VSYS, data on GP12/13. R13 resistor and bus resets handled automatically.

Cables
Any USB-A to Lightning cable works. Power the board via USB-C or 5V pin.
