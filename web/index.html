LED

LED states

Once flashed, the board runs completely on its own — no computer needed. Just power it up, plug in the iPhone, and watch the LED:

  • Solid Orange
    Idle, waiting for something to show up on USB.
  • Solid Magenta
    Recovery mode spotted. Hold Volume Down + Power now — board injects its reboot packet on tick 2.
  • Solid Cyan
    Let go of Power, keep holding Volume Down for about 8 seconds.
  • Blinking White
    Release everything. Waiting for DFU to enumerate.
  • Solid Blue
    USBLiter8 exploit is running.
  • Solid Green
    Payload uploaded, iPhone is booting. Done!
HW

Hardware notes

Waveshare RP2350 USB-A

The USB-A port feeds directly from VSYS (5V) and uses GP12/13 for data. The firmware handles the board's onboard R13 resistor automatically with active USB bus resets — no hardware mods needed.

Cables

Any standard USB-A to Lightning cable between the board's host port and the iPhone works. Power the board itself via USB-C or the 5V pin.
